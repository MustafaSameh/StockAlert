# Methodology: How StockAlert Calculates Reorder Points

StockAlert relies on statistical inventory control models to determine optimal reorder points. Instead of relying on gut feelings, it uses historical sales variance to build a mathematical safety net.

## The Core Variables

1. **Average Daily Sales ($\mu$):** The mean number of units sold per day.
2. **Standard Deviation of Sales ($\sigma$):** The measure of daily sales volatility. A high standard deviation means sales are erratic; a low one means sales are consistent.
3. **Lead Time ($L$):** The number of days it takes for a new order from a supplier to arrive in your inventory.
4. **Service Level ($Z$):** The desired probability of not hitting a stockout. StockAlert uses a **95% Service Level**, which translates to a Z-score of **1.65**.

## Step 1: Calculating Safety Stock

Safety Stock acts as a buffer against demand spikes or supplier delays. Because standard deviation scales with the square root of time (based on the variance of independent random variables), the formula is:

$$Safety Stock = Z \times \sigma \times \sqrt{L}$$

*Example: If your daily sales standard deviation is 5 units, lead time is 4 days, and Z-score is 1.65, your safety stock is `1.65 * 5 * 2 = 16.5` units.*

## Step 2: Calculating Lead Time Demand

This is the baseline amount of stock you will consume simply waiting for the delivery truck to arrive.

$$Lead Time Demand = \mu \times L$$

## Step 3: Final Reorder Point (ROP) Calculation

The ROP is the exact inventory level at which a new order should be placed. It is the sum of the Lead Time Demand and the Safety Stock buffer.

$$ROP = (\mu \times L) + (Z \times \sigma \times \sqrt{L})$$

## Advanced Forecasting (Prophet)

For the **Seasonal Forecasting** module, StockAlert utilizes Meta's Prophet model. Instead of looking purely at historical averages, Prophet models seasonal trends (weekly, yearly) and holiday effects. 

The forecasted future sales ($\hat{y}$) generated by Prophet are then fed back into the standard ROP equation to provide dynamic, forward-looking reorder points tailored to upcoming seasonal shifts.
